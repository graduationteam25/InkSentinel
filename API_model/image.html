PROBLEM STATEMENT

🎯
Core Challenge

Traditional signature verification is failing in the digital age: manual methods are slow, subjective, and vulnerable to sophisticated forgeries, exposing institutions to fraud, legal disputes, and identity theft risks.

⚙️
Technical Hurdles

Data Scarcity & Sensitivity
Authentic signature datasets are inherently confidential, requiring ethical curation, anonymization, and advanced preprocessing to protect privacy while enabling effective model training.
Environmental Variability
Factors such as inconsistent lighting, camera quality, and capture angles introduce noise that can significantly degrade verification accuracy in uncontrolled environments.
Performance Trade-offs
Achieving high accuracy with deep learning models requires balancing computational complexity against mobile device limitations (CPU/GPU/RAM) to ensure real-time performance.

⚠️
Industry Impact

Banking Sector
Banks face increased risk of loan and identity fraud.
Legal Professionals
Legal professionals risk contract disputes and evidence tampering.
Individual Users
Individuals are exposed to digital identity theft, impacting trust in remote transactions.
